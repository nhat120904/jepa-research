3. Research Topic
Tentative Title:
Counterfactual Action-Identifiable JEPA World Models for Robot Planning (CAI-JEPA).
Research Problem:
Do action-conditioned JEPA world models reliably distinguish the futures induced by different
actions across the diverse range of states encountered during robot planning, particularly in
contact-rich, fine-precision manipulation, and if not, how can this failure be systematically
diagnosed and corrected? Standard evaluation (factual prediction loss and aggregate planning
success) does not directly measure whether the predictor separates the outcomes of different
actions from the same initial state.
Main Objectives:
  1. Build a systematic quantitative diagnostic protocol for action grounding, stratified across
     state regimes (free space, pre-grasp, gripper actuation, contact manipulation).
  2. Establish how counterfactual sensitivity correlates with downstream planning success (the
     CRA_eff → planning-failure link).
  3. If failures are confirmed, design training objectives that explicitly enforce action-conditional
     separability in the predictor's latent space.

4. Research Progress
Key activities completed:
  • Implemented the full go/no-go diagnostic against the real facebookresearch/jepa-wms API
     (frozen, pretrained checkpoints only, nothing is trained)
  • Defined and validated four per-transition metrics — Counterfactual Ranking Accuracy
    (CRA), Action Usage Gap (AUG), Effect-Conditioned Separability (ECS), and optional
    CTD, with trajectory-clustered bootstrap confidence intervals.
  • Built regime stratification (free_space, pre_grasp, gripper_actuation,
    contact_manipulation) and the dataset/task/regime/strategy evaluation matrix, plus a
    synthetic-model validation path.
  • Completed the primary Metaworld study end-to-end (latent extraction, regime
    classification, diagnostic run, analysis report).
  • Set up, downloaded, and cached the secondary DROID study: environment built, 333-
    episode subset downloaded, latents encoded (dino_wm_droid), and regimes recalibrated.
  • Coded the planning Action-Score probe (faithful CEM planner + DROID Action Error
    metric) to close the link between counterfactual sensitivity and planning failure.
Preliminary results / outputs:
  • Consolidated decision: CONDITIONAL_GO. DROID is strongly pathological and
     Metaworld is clearly below the abandon threshold, though not below the stricter full-GO
     threshold.
  • Metaworld: a large strategy gap. "opposite" (easy) counterfactuals are near-saturated
     (CRA ≈ 0.96–0.99), but strict "hard_nn" negatives drop to ≈ 0.46–0.57 in pre-grasp and
     contact regimes; hard-task contact-regime CRA_eff ≈ 0.65 (upper CI 0.70). The model
     reacts to gross action changes but struggles when a counterfactual action is paired with a
     similar latent state.
  • DROID: after the sanity gate passes, strict hard negatives are near chance (CRA_eff ≈
     0.02–0.07) in gripper_actuation and contact_manipulation => the sharpest action-
     grounding failure observed.
  • Outputs: diagnosis result, the decision report, and per-regime figures.
