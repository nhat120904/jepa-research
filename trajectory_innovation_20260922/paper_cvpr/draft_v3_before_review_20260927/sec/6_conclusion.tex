\paragraph{Limitations.}
\method is not label-free: task-progress labels from the simulator train the reader and the ranking term of the world model, although nothing uses labels at test time.
The proposal policy is pretrained on expert demonstrations.
Training data require branching the simulator at every decision.
A selector cannot exceed the best candidate in the bank, so gains are bounded by the policy's proposals.
