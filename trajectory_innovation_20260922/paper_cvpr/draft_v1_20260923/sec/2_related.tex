\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models for visual planning.}
Latent world models learn dynamics in a compact state space and plan or learn behaviors in imagination~\cite{hafner2019planet,hafner2023dreamerv3,hansen2024tdmpc2}.
Token-based models cast dynamics as autoregressive sequence modeling over discrete image tokens~\cite{micheli2023iris}, and $\Delta$-IRIS reduces their cost by encoding only the stochastic change between consecutive frames given the past~\cite{micheli2024deltairis}.
DINO-WM~\cite{zhou2025dinowm} and V-JEPA~2-AC~\cite{assran2025vjepa2} predict in the patch-feature space of a pretrained encoder~\cite{oquab2024dinov2,bardes2024vjepa} and plan by minimizing the feature distance between the predicted final frame and a goal image.
CompACT~\cite{kim2026compact} compresses each observation into as few as eight discrete tokens to accelerate such planning, and GCQ~\cite{peng2025gcq} compresses observation--action sequences with an action-conditioned codebook.
Almost all of these models are trained to reproduce observations, and they are queried by rolling out one step per frame.
\method differs in its unit of prediction and in its training signal.
It predicts one code per action segment, conditioned on the history available to the reader, and the code is shaped by the questions a planner asks and by its predictability from actions rather than by reconstruction of observations.
Compact per-frame codes are therefore our most important control: a per-frame code with the same context and the same total number of bits must not match \method if the segment-level claim is to hold.

\paragraph{Temporally extended prediction.}
Option models~\cite{sutton1999options} and multi-step or trajectory-level dynamics models~\cite{lambert2021longterm,ding2024dwm} predict the outcome of a temporally extended action without stepping through every intermediate state, and jumpy world models compose such predictions across timescales~\cite{farebrother2026jumpy}.
Successor features and their random-feature variants predict cumulative features of a policy or an action sequence~\cite{barreto2017sf,chen2023ramp}, and TD-Flow learns generative models of discounted future-state occupancy~\cite{farebrother2025tdflow}.
These representations summarize the future either as states at a given time or as order-free aggregates.
\method instead retains ordered, non-additive information about a segment, such as whether one event precedes another, and it conditions the summary on the observed history rather than predicting it from scratch.

\paragraph{Policy steering with world models.}
A frozen generative policy can be improved at deployment by sampling several action proposals and selecting one with an external scorer.
GPC~\cite{qi2025gpc} ranks diffusion-policy proposals by task reward predicted on the rollouts of an action-conditioned world model.
FOREWARN~\cite{wu2025forewarn} aligns a vision--language model to latent predictions of candidate plans.
DreamSteer~\cite{cui2026dreamsteer} ranks imagined latent futures of VLA chunks with a language-conditioned value model, and DynaGuide~\cite{du2025dynaguide} steers denoising with a DINOv2-space dynamics model.
Value- or verifier-based methods rank proposals without a world model~\cite{nakamoto2024vgps,kwok2025robomonkey}.
We adopt the same propose-then-select interface, with a frozen policy and a shared proposal bank for every compared model, but our contribution concerns what the world model predicts.
Our scores come from self-supervised queries on predicted codes rather than from rewards, success labels, or a vision--language model.

\paragraph{Conditional coding and decision-aware compression.}
Learned image and video codecs optimize a rate--distortion trade-off end to end~\cite{balle2017end}, and DCVC~\cite{li2021dcvc} replaces residual coding with conditional coding, in which both encoder and decoder use the temporal context.
Unlike the Wyner--Ziv setting~\cite{wyner1976ratedistortion}, where side information is available only to the decoder, our encoder and reader both see the context.
In reinforcement learning, value-equivalent models~\cite{grimm2020value,schrittwieser2020muzero} retain only what is needed to predict values, and value-equivalent sampling~\cite{arumugam2022deciding} formalizes decision-relevant lossy model compression.
\method is an instance of these principles with two differences.
Its source is the unexecuted future of an action, which must also be predicted before execution; predictability is therefore an explicit term of the objective.
In addition, its distortion is measured on an open family of self-supervised visual--temporal queries rather than on the values of a fixed set of policies.
