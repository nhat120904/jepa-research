\paragraph{Limitations.}
The reader is trained with task-progress labels from the simulator, so \method is not label-free, although the world model never sees a label and nothing uses labels at test time.
The proposal policy is a pretrained imitation-learning model, so the complete system is not free of expert data.
Training data require branching the simulator at every decision.
PushT has a single fixed goal and cannot test transfer across goals; that claim rests on LIBERO-Goal.
Our LIBERO-Goal policy reproduces below its reported success rate.
