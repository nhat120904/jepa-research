\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Latent and joint-embedding world models.}
Latent world models learn dynamics in a compact state space and plan or learn behaviors in imagination~\cite{hafner2019planet,hafner2023dreamerv3,hansen2024tdmpc2}.
Token-based models cast dynamics as autoregressive modeling of discrete image tokens~\cite{micheli2023iris}.
$\Delta$-IRIS encodes only the stochastic change between consecutive frames given the past~\cite{micheli2024deltairis}, which makes it the closest per-step relative of conditional coding.
Joint-embedding predictive architectures predict the representation of a target from the representation of its context instead of reconstructing the target~\cite{lecun2022path,assran2023ijepa,bardes2024vjepa}.
In control, DINO-WM~\cite{zhou2025dinowm} and V-JEPA~2-AC~\cite{assran2025vjepa2} predict the patch features of a frozen encoder~\cite{oquab2024dinov2} one time step at a time, and they plan by minimizing the feature distance between a predicted latent state and a goal image.
CompACT~\cite{kim2026compact} compresses each observation into as few as eight tokens to accelerate such planning, and GCQ~\cite{peng2025gcq} compresses observation--action sequences with an action-conditioned codebook.
In all of these models the prediction target is fixed in advance: the observation at each time step, or a per-step latent representation of it.
\method keeps the joint-embedding recipe, a predictor trained against the stop-gradient output of a target encoder, but learns the target itself.
The target is one code per action segment, conditioned on history that the reader also sees, and shaped by what decisions need.
Compact per-step codes are therefore our most important control.

\paragraph{Temporally extended prediction.}
Option models~\cite{sutton1999options} and multi-step or trajectory-level dynamics models~\cite{lambert2021longterm,ding2024dwm} predict the outcome of a temporally extended action without stepping through every intermediate state, and jumpy world models compose such predictions across timescales~\cite{farebrother2026jumpy}.
Successor features and their random-feature variants predict cumulative features of a policy or an action sequence~\cite{barreto2017sf,chen2023ramp}, and TD-Flow learns generative models of discounted future-state occupancy~\cite{farebrother2025tdflow}.
These models summarize the future as a state at a given time or as an order-free aggregate, and they predict it from scratch.
\method codes the whole segment, including its intermediate frames, and spends its bits only on what the observed history does not already determine.

\paragraph{Policy steering with world models.}
A frozen generative policy can be improved at deployment by sampling several proposals and selecting one with an external scorer.
GPC~\cite{qi2025gpc} ranks diffusion-policy proposals with a reward model applied to the future observations predicted by an action-conditioned world model; it is the closest system to ours.
FOREWARN~\cite{wu2025forewarn} aligns a vision--language model to latent predictions of candidate plans.
DreamSteer~\cite{cui2026dreamsteer} ranks imagined latent futures of VLA chunks with a language-conditioned value model.
DynaGuide~\cite{du2025dynaguide} steers denoising with a DINOv2-space dynamics model.
Value- or verifier-based methods rank proposals without a world model~\cite{nakamoto2024vgps,kwok2025robomonkey}.
We adopt the same propose-then-select interface and, like GPC and V-GPS, train the scorer with task-progress labels.
Our contribution concerns what the world model predicts.
It predicts a goal-independent code that one reader evaluates for any goal image, where GPC predicts future observations that a reward model must read and a direct scorer outputs a score that must be recomputed for every goal.

\paragraph{Conditional coding and decision-aware compression.}
Learned image and video codecs optimize a rate--distortion trade-off end to end~\cite{balle2017end}.
DCVC~\cite{li2021dcvc} replaces residual coding with conditional coding, in which both encoder and decoder use the temporal context.
Unlike the Wyner--Ziv setting~\cite{wyner1976ratedistortion}, where side information is available only to the decoder, our encoder and heads both see the context.
In reinforcement learning, value-equivalent models~\cite{grimm2020value,schrittwieser2020muzero} retain only what is needed to predict values, and value-equivalent sampling~\cite{arumugam2022deciding} formalizes decision-relevant lossy model compression.
\method differs in two ways.
Its source is the unexecuted future of an action, which must be predicted before execution, so predictability is part of the design.
Its distortion combines a decision term, the ranking of candidates toward a goal, with a generic term, the reconstruction of the future's features, so that the code is not specialized to the goals seen in training.
